Across the slope: left is the flat top's edge, right is the face's edge. Up is light, down is dark. Click to add a point, drag to move, double-click a point to remove it (two stay).

Colours

Tick a colour to set it; the body colour (Parts above) is the knob's cap. The slope follows the body, and the highlight and shadow keep their softness.

Pointer

Highlight: the lit band along the rim's top-left (0 is none). Cap size: the flat top as a fraction of the face, so it scales with the knob. Slope shade: how strong the light and dark sides of the taper are. Pointer length: how far the tip reaches (1 is full). Offset: move the pointer out towards the rim (+) or in towards the centre (-).
